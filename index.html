ARJUN BHIMAVARAPU · CREATIVE PRODUCER

I work at the intersection of storytelling, production and digital creativity. From being behind the camera to shaping the final edit, I care about how a story feels as much as how it looks.

My experience spans videography, video editing, creative leadership and freelance projects for brands and corporate clients. I enjoy taking an idea from the first conversation to the final frame.

I'm also the founder of OopsCut Media — a creative venture built around making thoughtful visual content for brands and businesses.
